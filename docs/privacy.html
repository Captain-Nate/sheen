Sheen: Bubble Pop

Privacy Policy · Effective July 23, 2026

The short version: Sheen collects no data.

Data collection

Sheen does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It works completely offline.

Game progress

Your progress (coins, level stars, and unlocked themes) is saved only on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this local data.

In-app purchases

Optional theme purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details or any personal information from the transaction. Apple's own privacy policy governs the purchase process; purchases can be restored on any device signed into your Apple ID.

Children

Sheen is rated 4+ and, since it collects no data at all, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future update adds an online feature), the updated policy will be posted at this address before the change takes effect.
