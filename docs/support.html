Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the Shop and tap Restore at the top of the sheet. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored for free on any of your devices.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No — Sheen works completely offline. A connection is only used by Apple when you make or restore a purchase.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes local progress (coins, stars, unlocked-by-play themes). Paid themes can always be restored.
